# SKYLINE PLAZA (branch `feat/skyline-plaza`)

The SKYLINE ONE concept tower, a CITE BIRD showcase, is placed in Fyrus as a landmark plaza with a walk-in interior. SKYLINE ONE is a fictional building: there is no developer, site or sale behind it, and the in-world sign says so.

## What changed

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `fyrus-skyline.js` (new) | Add-on module that builds the plaza and tower, the entrance interaction, the interior overlay and all world hooks. |
| `assets/skyline/skyline-one-tower.glb` (new, 2.8 MB) | SKYLINE ONE exterior geometry and materials, exported from the SKYLINE ONE app. It is merged per material: 33 meshes, 38,676 triangles, JPEG textures. It is converted for the r149-era `fyrus-gltf-loader.js`, so it has no instancing or emissive-strength extensions. |
| `assets/skyline-one/` (new, 4.7 MB) | The SKYLINE ONE app build with a relative base. It is opened as `index.html?embed=fyrus`, with the same origin and no server change. `.md` licences are mirrored as `.txt`, because `server.mjs` refuses `*.md`. |
| `src/client/template.html` | Adds one `<script src="/fyrus-skyline.js?v=1.0.0" defer>` after the airport module. |
| `src/client/app/023.js` | Inserts one drone-tour stop, "SKYLINE PLAZA — SKYLINE ONE", between Starter Commons and Executive Enclave. The waypoint is (360, 65, 320) and the camera looks at the tower (295, 30, 462). It also adds optional per-stop `look` support, which no other stop uses. |
| `index.html` | Regenerated by `node build.mjs`. |

Not touched: `server.mjs` (the build output is byte-identical), airports, aircraft, gates, terminals, other plazas, users, balances, the database and environment variables.

## Location

- **Plaza centre:** (295, 462). It is rotated π, so the entrance faces north, towards Fyrus Core.
- **Tower footprint:** x 277.8–312.2, z 443.1–475.4. The height is 63 m.
- **Door / gold ring:** (295, 441).
- **Plaza slab:** x 259–331, z 402–484.
- **"Where to?" entry:** "SKYLINE PLAZA" lands at (295, 406), facing the tower.

Airport clearance:

- About 230 m west of the FCI runway centreline (x ≈ 527).
- 111 m from the nearest FCI blocked zone (384, 395).
- Outside the FCI footprint.
- No taxiway, gate, jet bridge or aircraft path crosses the plaza.

## Hooks

All hooks are chained and none are replaced:

- `W.blocked`: 9 zones. They stop procedural blocks from regenerating on the plaza.
- `W.traffic.blocked`: cars do not route across the plaza.
- `__fyrusCollide`: a box for the tower, plus the fountain, sign, palms and lamps.
- `__fyrusSolid`, `__fyrusNoProps` and `__fyrusRegion` (key `skyline-plaza`).
- `__fyrusMinimap`: gold plaza and tower.
- `__fyrusCamera`: the third-person camera is pulled out of the tower box.
- `__fyrusExtraInteracts`: "Enter SKYLINE ONE" at the door.
- `__fyrusPlaces`: a property hook keeps the entry when `fyrus-country.js` reassigns the list.

Build and dispose rules:

- The plaza is built when the player **or** the camera is within 650 m, so the drone tour sees it.
- It is disposed beyond 950 m. GPU buffers are released, and the parsed GLB stays cached for a quick return.
- While the interior overlay is open, the city render loop is paused. It is restored on exit.

## Kill switches and debugging

- `?skyline=off` disables the whole module.
- `?skypause=0` keeps the city rendering behind the interior overlay.
- `window.__fyrusSkyline.stats()` returns debug stats.

## Rollback

- **Before merge:** delete the branch. Nothing on `main` changes.
- **After merge/deploy:** `git revert <merge commit>` and push, or redeploy the previous image in Coolify.
- **Quick runtime disable without a deploy:** none. The module only exists in the new build, so a revert is the rollback.
