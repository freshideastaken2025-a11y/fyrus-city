SKYLINE ONE

Explore the building. Experience every floor.

An original 15-storey residential tower, created by CITE BIRD to show what an interactive 3D sales experience can do: orbit the tower, open any floor, walk through furnished residences, read the floor plans and take the guided tour — in the browser, with nothing to install.

  • Architectural floor-plan interpretation
  • Interactive 3D property exploration
  • Interior & exterior visualisation
  • Digital property showrooms
  • Guided walkthroughs & sales presentations

Concept demonstration. SKYLINE ONE is not a real building, client project, approved plan or property for sale. All plans, dimensions and areas are illustrative.

3D credits: velvet sofa and potted plant models by Eric Chadwick / Wayfair LLC / Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH, CC BY 4.0; chair and vase models CC0 — via Khronos glTF Sample Assets. Asset licences. All other geometry and materials are original CITE BIRD work.
